# Design Pillars

## 1. Ecology Over Traditional Fantasy
The world operates through:
- nutrient cycles
- predation
- parasitism
- decomposition
- adaptation
- symbiosis

Magic is biological rather than arcane.

---

## 2. Complexity Through Interactions
Complexity emerges from:
- resource dependencies
- environmental pressure
- overlapping systems
- cross-kingdom synergies

Avoid isolated mechanics.

---

## 3. Distinct Kingdom Playstyles
Each kingdom should feel fundamentally different.

Examples:
- Plants = passive territorial growth
- Fungi = network expansion/control
- Insects = swarm logistics
- Predators = active combat
- Parasites = infection and attrition

---

## 4. Prestige As Evolution
Prestige resets represent:
- evolutionary advancement
- ecological succession
- biological discovery

Players unlock:
- new kingdoms
- hybrid systems
- symbiotic mechanics
- permanent adaptations

---

## 5. Mobile-Friendly Tempo
Gameplay alternates between:
- passive macro progression
- short active gameplay bursts

Sessions should work well in:
- 2 minute check-ins
- 10 minute active play sessions
