# Kingdom Definitions

## Plantae

### Gameplay Style
- passive growth
- territory expansion
- sunlight management
- root networks

### Core Resources
- sunlight
- water
- nutrients

### Active Gameplay
- toxin blooms
- rapid growth bursts
- herbivore defense

### Strengths
- efficient energy generation
- strong passive scaling

### Weaknesses
- immobility
- vulnerable expansion phase

---

## Fungi

### Gameplay Style
- decomposition
- underground spread
- parasitism
- nutrient redistribution

### Core Resources
- decay
- spores
- biomass

### Active Gameplay
- outbreak events
- infection chains
- corpse harvesting

### Strengths
- map-wide influence
- attrition mechanics

### Weaknesses
- weak direct combat
- dependence on biomass

---

## Symbiosis

### Gameplay Style
Combined plant + fungal systems.

Examples:
- shared nutrient networks
- growth amplification
- hybrid abilities
- distributed territory bonuses

### Design Goal
The first major “complexity leap” in the game.
